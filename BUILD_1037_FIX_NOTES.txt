Material Library Form Consistency Fix

Changes:
- The Material Library Add Material workflow now requests the complete material record in one pass:
  Material Name, Category, Unit Cost, Supplier, SKU, and Notes.
- Editing a material now uses the same complete field sequence.
- Scenario Builder and Material Library therefore collect the same material information.
- Existing material records, pricing calculations, projects, imports, exports, and storage keys remain compatible.

Test:
1. Open Material Library and choose Add Material.
2. Confirm all six fields are requested before the material is saved.
3. Edit the saved material and confirm the same six fields are available.
4. Open Scenario Builder and confirm the material is available in the Material dropdown.
