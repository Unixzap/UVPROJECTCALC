BUILD 1042 RC2 PRICING VERIFICATION

Canonical rules verified in source:
1. Production cost includes quantity-aware blank and packaging costs.
2. Automatic quote uses target-margin division, not markup multiplication.
3. Profit = Quote Before Tax - Production Cost.
4. Achieved Margin = Profit / Quote Before Tax.
5. Sales Tax = Quote Before Tax × Tax Rate.
6. Customer Quote = Quote Before Tax + Sales Tax.
7. Dashboard, Projects, Reports, CSV, and Scenario Builder use canonical pricing snapshots.

Reference test:
Production Cost $25.65, target margin 50%, rounding upward to $1, tax 5%.
