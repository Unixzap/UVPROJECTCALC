ADDED OFFICIAL SPECIFICATIONS

- Maximum print resolution: 720 × 1440 dpi
- Pixel-Scan Vision System resolution: 1440 × 1200 dpi
- Positioning accuracy: ±0.2 mm
- Maximum embossed print height: 7 mm (0.28 in)
- Main-unit dimensions: 28.1 × 14.7 × 18.4 in
  (714 × 374 × 468.5 mm)
- Single UV weight: 89.5 lb (40.6 kg)
- Dual UV and UV + DT weights: 90.7 lb (41.12 kg)
- Supported operating systems: macOS and Windows
- Remote app cleaning and control
- Automatic white-ink stirring and circulation
- Automatic printhead moisture protection

WORKFLOW AND MATERIAL CLARITY

- Single UV: basic rigid materials, direct UV, UV DTF with a laminator,
  varnish effects, and 3D texture.
- Dual UV: rigid and compatible flexible materials, fluorescent effects,
  rigid/flexible white, UV DTF with a laminator, varnish, and 3D texture.
- UV + DT: rigid and fabric work with distinct Direct UV, DTG, DTF fabric,
  UV DTF with a laminator, varnish, and 3D texture workflows.
- Added edition-specific material descriptions.
