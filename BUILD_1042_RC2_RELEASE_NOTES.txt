UNIVERSAL PROJECT CALCULATOR v1.0.1 RC2 — BUILD 1042

FEATURE FREEZE INTEGRATION RELEASE

Integrated cumulative corrections from Builds 1028 through 1041:
- Live recalculation for quantity, tax, and pricing controls.
- Quantity-aware blank and packaging costs.
- Canonical pricing snapshot used by editor, project cards, Dashboard, Reports, CSV, and Scenario Builder.
- Target Profit Margin pricing: Quote Before Tax = Production Cost / (1 - Target Margin).
- Sales tax added after the quote and excluded from profit and margin.
- Saved projects reopen from their stored pricing inputs/snapshot without hidden manual-price locking.
- Material Library naming and unified add/edit workflow.
- Scenario Builder uses Material as its product selector and includes Add New Material.
- Sample business data retained.
- Templates feature removed from navigation, Project Calculator, settings export, and application workflows.
