Universal Project Calculator v1.0.1 — Build 1032

PRICING METHOD CONFIRMED
- Automatic pricing uses Option B: target profit margin.
- Formula: Quote Before Tax = Production Cost / (1 - Target Margin).
- Profit and margin exclude sales tax.

CLARITY IMPROVEMENTS
- Renamed project input to Target Profit Margin (%).
- Renamed the default setting to Default Target Profit Margin %.
- Cost Breakdown now shows Blank Cost as quantity × cost per unit.
- Cost Breakdown now shows Packaging as quantity × packaging per unit.
- Cost Breakdown now shows Total Job Ink as mL × ink rate per mL.
- Customer Quote section identifies the Target Margin pricing method and target percentage.

TEST CASE CONFIRMED
- Production Cost: $25.65
- 50% target margin gives an unrounded quote of $51.30.
- With $1 upward rounding, Quote Before Tax is $52.00.
- 5% sales tax is $2.60.
- Customer Quote is $54.60.
