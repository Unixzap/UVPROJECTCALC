Universal Project Calculator v1.0.1 — Build 1048
Customers Library List and Sorting

Purpose:
- Improve Customers Library usability for businesses with large customer lists.

Changes:
- Replaced the Customers Library card layout with a compact table/list view.
- Added a search field that filters by customer name, customer type, email address, or discount.
- Added sortable Customer Name, Customer Type, Email, and Discount columns.
- Clicking an active column heading reverses the sort direction.
- Added a visible result count showing filtered and total customer records.
- Preserved Edit and Delete actions on every customer row.
- Added responsive customer rows for narrow screens and mobile devices.
- Customer email addresses remain available and are now clickable mail links.
- Updated visible build labels, script cache-busters, and the service-worker cache key to Build 1048.

Not Changed:
- Customer Add/Edit/Delete dialogs and validation.
- Customer IDs, stored records, or browser storage format.
- Customer import/export formats.
- Email Quote customer lookup behavior.
- Pricing engine, pricing formulas, tax calculations, or pricing snapshots.
- Material Library, Scenario Builder, Projects, Dashboard, Reports, or sample business data.

Testing:
1. Open Customers Library and confirm customers appear in a compact table.
2. Search by customer name, type, email, and discount.
3. Sort each column in ascending and descending order.
4. Edit and delete customers while the list is searched or sorted; confirm the correct record is affected.
5. Add a customer and confirm it appears in the current sorted list.
6. Resize to a narrow/mobile viewport and confirm each customer remains readable and actionable.
7. Create an Email Quote and confirm the stored customer email is still selected.

Regression Focus:
- Customers Library Add/Edit/Delete.
- Customer import/export and saved-data compatibility.
- Email Quote customer email lookup.
- Existing pricing and project calculations.
