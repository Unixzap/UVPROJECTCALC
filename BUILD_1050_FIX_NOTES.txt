MAIN PRICING WORKSPACE

- Added configured Overhead / Hour to production cost.
- Added configured Service Reserve / Hour to production cost.
- Added configured Cleaning Allowance / Job to fixed job cost.
- Added configured Default Shipping / Order to fixed job cost.
- Renamed the project-level "Primer & Cleaning" field to "Primer / Pretreatment"

- Expanded the visible cost breakdown so all applied costs are shown.
- New and edited projects now snapshot ink, labor, machine, electricity,
  maintenance, overhead, shipping, cleaning, and service-reserve rates.

PRICING FORMULA

- Quantity-based costs:
  blanks = quantity × blank cost per unit
  packaging = quantity × packaging cost per unit
- Job-based costs:
  ink = total job ink × ink cost per mL
  labor = hands-on hours × labor rate
  machine = print hours × machine rate
  electricity = print hours × electricity rate
  overhead = (print hours + hands-on hours) × overhead rate
  service reserve = print hours × service-reserve rate
  maintenance, cleaning, primer, shipping, and other costs are applied per job.
- Waste is applied to the complete production-cost base.
- Target-margin price remains:
  production cost ÷ (1 − target margin)
- Sales tax remains excluded from profit and margin calculations.

INPUT SAFETY

- Added calculation-level guards in addition to the existing HTML controls.
- Negative cost, time, rate, and price values are treated as zero.
- Quantity is constrained to a whole number of at least one.
- Waste, target margin, discount, and sales tax are constrained to their
  supported ranges.
- Invalid or non-positive rounding values safely fall back to one cent.
- Target margin remains capped at 95 percent to prevent division by zero.
