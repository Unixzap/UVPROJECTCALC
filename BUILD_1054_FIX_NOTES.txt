SUPPORTED EDITIONS

1. Single UV Edition
   Best for: Entry-level rigid-material personalization
   Print head: 1 × Epson F1080
   Resolution: 720 × 1440 dpi
   Working area: 13 × 4.8 in standard; 13 × 16.5 in large
   Ink: CMYKWW plus 3-in-1 varnish
   Capacity: 125 mL standard ink

2. Dual-Head UV Edition
   Best for: Advanced UV applications and neon effects
   Print heads: 2 × Epson F1080
   Resolution: 720 × 1440 dpi
   Working area: 13 × 4.8 in standard; 13 × 16.5 in large
   Ink: CMYKWW, rigid white, flexible white, fluorescent red, fluorescent
        yellow, and 3-in-1 varnish
   Capacity: 125 mL standard ink; 290 mL high-capacity white

3. UV + DT Fabric Edition
   Best for: Apparel and hard-goods printing
   Print heads: 2 × Epson F1080
   Resolution: 720 × 1440 dpi
   Working area: 13 × 4.8 in standard; 13 × 16.5 in large;
                 11.8 × 15.4 in DTG platen
   Ink: UV CMYKWWDT, fabric CMYKWW, and 3-in-1 varnish
   Capacity: 125 mL standard ink; 290 mL high-capacity white
