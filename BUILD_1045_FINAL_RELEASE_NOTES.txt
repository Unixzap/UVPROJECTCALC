UNIVERSAL PROJECT CALCULATOR v1.0.1 — BUILD 1045
FINAL RELEASE

EMAIL QUOTE WORKFLOW

Changes:
- Added an Email Quote action to every saved project card.
- Added a styled quote-email preview matching the application UI.
- Automatically fills the customer email when it exists in Customers Library.
- Generates a customer-safe message containing quote reference, project, quantity, quote before tax, sales tax, total quote, validity period, business terms, and business contact information.
- Opens the completed draft in the user’s default email program using a mailto link.
- The application does not send email directly and does not collect or store email credentials.
- Users may edit the recipient, subject, and message before opening the email program.

RELEASE SAFETY
- Pricing formulas and canonical pricing snapshots were not changed.
- Production cost, profit, and margin are not included in the customer email.
- Customer, project, material, import/export, and storage schemas were not changed.
- Visible version labels and offline cache identifiers advanced to Version 1.0.1 Build 1045.

Testing:
1. Open Projects and select Email Quote on a project with a saved customer email.
2. Confirm To, Subject, and Message are populated.
3. Confirm Quote Before Tax, Sales Tax, and Total Quote match the project card.
4. Confirm no production cost, profit, or margin appears in the customer message.
5. Choose Open Email and confirm the default email program opens with the draft populated.
6. Repeat with a customer that has no email; the email program should open with the To field available for entry.
